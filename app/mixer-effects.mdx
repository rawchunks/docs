Focus a lane and open **MIXER**. Its channel badge cycles the target. The fader adjusts level; **PAN** sets left/right position. **BASS**, **MID**, and **HIGH** cover **−12 to +12 dB**. Double-click controls to reset them.

A selected Master range adds **TRIM** for its level. Check the whole song after tone changes; the section's EQ controls affect Master tone beyond that range.

## Add effects

Open **FX**. Check the target badge, choose a module, enable **ON**, then adjust it while listening. EQ starts enabled; other effect modules start bypassed.

| Tab | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| **WAV** | [Wave sculpting](/app/wave-sculpting) |
| **EQ** | Low, Mid, High gain |
| **FLT** | Filter type, cutoff, resonance |
| **DRV** | Drive and dry/wet blend |
| **CHR** | Chorus rate, depth, blend |
| **DLY** | Delay time, feedback, blend |
| **REV** | Reverb decay and blend |
| **CMP** | Compressor threshold, ratio, attack, release |
| **3D** | Spatial azimuth, elevation, distance |

Enable **FLT** before using Mixer's filter cutoff. Delay presets use fixed times, so check them against the project tempo.

## Place a sound in 3D

Choose **3D**, turn it **ON**, then set **AZIMUTH** for direction around you, **ELEVATION** for height, and **DISTANCE** for how far away the sound sits. Listen on headphones while positioning it.

<Frame caption="Example project in the current Rawchunks app.">

Master effects also affect takes mounted on it. Ordinary lanes have their own settings. [Source extraction](/app/extract-separate#extract-a-section) does not bake these effects into its slice.
