Open **MARKET** for public originals and remixes. Cards show the title, creator, genre tags, listening rate, and licensing information.

<Frame caption="Example project in the current Rawchunks app.">
  <img src="/images/app/market.jpg" alt="Current Market with search, creator and genre filters, date sorting, and compact song cards." />
</Frame>

Search by title, tags, or creator address. Use the **creator** and **genre** dropdowns, or select a card's genre tag. Sort **newest** or **oldest**. Reset the filters to return to the full catalog.

Use the card's play icon to stream it. Listening to another creator's music requires an [active session](/app/payments) and uses its listed **$/MIN** rate. Your own tracks are exempt.

## Use the bottom player

The player stays available while you move between Studio, My Page, and Market. Use play/pause, **−5s / +5s**, or the progress bar to seek. The time display shows elapsed and total duration; **VOL** sets volume on desktop.

The editor has a separate [arrangement transport](/app/editor#select-and-listen).

## Remix an original

Select the license tag icon, **Buy License**, to review the commercial license. After purchase, open **MY-PAGE → MY LICENSES** and use the remix icon. See [licenses and remixes](/app/licenses).

A **REMIX** badge identifies a derivative track; remixes cannot be licensed again in the app.
