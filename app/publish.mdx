Publishing mints an unminted version and lists it in **MARKET** for listening and licensing.

## Choose selling terms

Studio's **FINANCIALS** sets the starting terms for a new original:

| Field | What you set | Default |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| **STREAM ($/MIN)** | The listening rate | $0.01/min |
| **LICENSE ($)** | Commercial remix license price | $5 |
| **LICENSES** | Licenses offered | 20 |

These terms are separate from [generation fees](/app/payments). You can revise them in **MY SONGS → ••• → SONG DETAILS** before the version is minted or public.

## Make a track public

1. Select the version you intend to publish.
2. Connect its creator account and choose the appropriate network.
3. Select the upload icon beside **PRIVATE** in My Songs, or **MAKE PUBLIC** on a Studio variation.
4. Review **CONFIRM PUBLICATION**, including the track, account, stream rate, license count, and price.
5. Select **CONFIRM & PUBLISH** and complete the required prompts. Wait for confirmation before looking for it in Market.

<MintNotice />

An original's mint records its creator, metadata, licensing terms, and stream rate. A remix's mint links it to the original on the same network. Clear the rights to your recordings and lyrics before publishing.

## Keep working

Editing a minted song creates a new editable version. Publish or download that version when it is ready; it has its own mint.

Where a **MAKE PRIVATE** control is offered, it removes the Market listing. The onchain record and public metadata remain.

See [licenses](/app/licenses) for the creator's share of a license sale and the remix workflow.
