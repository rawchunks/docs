## Extend

Choose **EXTEND → LENGTH** in AI Studio. The presets show both added time and the new total: **+30s (78s)** adds 30 seconds to a 48-second source. The slider sets the total target, up to **300 seconds**, and must exceed the current length.

Clear the range selection to continue at the ending. With a selected range ending earlier in the song, the continuation is inserted after that endpoint and the later audio moves forward.

Enter a continuation prompt and optional **LYRICS**. Choose **Flexible**, **Detailed**, or **Fast**, then **EXTEND**.

## Restyle

Choose **RESTYLE** and describe the new arrangement: “Slow acoustic arrangement with brushed drums,” for example.

Open **BLEND**. The presets are **Subtle (35%)**, **Balanced (65%)**, **Bold (85%)**, and **Full Cover (100%)**. The slider covers **5–100%**. Higher values request a larger style change.

Focus a lane or Master and select a range if needed. Choose **Flexible**, **Detailed**, or **Fast**, then **RESTYLE**. Open **LYRICS** to change the words.
