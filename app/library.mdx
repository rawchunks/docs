**MY-PAGE** holds **MY SONGS**, **MY LICENSES**, **MY ALBUMS**, and **MY VOICES**. Connect the account that owns your work. Each section can be collapsed.

## Choose a song and open its menu

Use numbered version pills when offered to choose a version. Open the card's **•••** menu for its actions.

<Frame caption="Example project in the current Rawchunks app.">
  <img src="/images/app/library-menu.jpg" alt="My Songs with compact cards and the Song Details, Audio Editor, Download, and Delete menu." />
</Frame>

**AUDIO EDITOR** continues an unminted version directly. Editing a minted song creates another editable version.

## Edit song details

For a private, unminted track, select **SONG DETAILS**. Change its title, genre, artwork, stream price, license allocation, and license price, then **SAVE DETAILS**.

<Frame caption="Example project in the current Rawchunks app.">
  <img src="/images/app/library-details.jpg" alt="Edit Song Details dialog with title, genre, artwork, stream price, total licenses, and license price." />
</Frame>

Review these terms before [publishing](/app/publish). The menu no longer offers Song Details once that version is public or minted.

## Download a song

Select **••• → DOWNLOAD**. Complete any required mint or account-activation prompts. The browser downloads a title-based WAV file.

<MintNotice />

A download can mint a version while its listing remains **PRIVATE**. If the file download fails after minting, retrying does not undo the mint.

## Publish or delete

The upload icon beside **PRIVATE** opens the [publication review](/app/publish#make-a-track-public). Review it before confirming.

**••• → DELETE** deletes that version immediately. The app has no confirmation or recovery control for this action.

**MY LICENSES** holds [licensed originals](/app/licenses#open-a-remix) still in the public catalog. **MY ALBUMS** holds [album cards](/app/covers-albums); **MY VOICES** holds [saved vocal profiles](/app/voices).
