title: "Build a drum pattern"
sidebarTitle: "Drums"
description: "Program or record a repeating five-voice, 16-step beat."
---

Open **DRUMS** in the manual dock or **+N** menu. Create a drum track if prompted, then **OPEN DRUM MATRIX**.

<Frame caption="Example project in the current Rawchunks app.">
  <img src="/images/app/editor-drums.jpg" alt="Expanded Drums editor with Kick, Snare, Clap, Hi-Hat, Perc, sixteen steps, Pad Rec, and version controls." />
</Frame>

1. Select steps in **KICK**, **SNARE**, **CLAP**, **HI-HAT**, and **PERC** to toggle hits. Select a row label to hear that sound.
2. Use **1–5** or **Q W E R T** as keyboard pads. Enable **PAD REC** to record at the cursor, or at the playhead during playback. Left/right arrows move the step cursor.
3. Use **RANDOMIZE** for another pattern or the clear icon to empty it.
4. Play the arrangement to hear the one-bar pattern repeat. Set its tempo with the header's **BPM** badge.

The compact preview shows one voice at a time; its dropdown chooses the row. Numbered pills switch drum versions and **+** starts a fresh one. A separated drum lane can also offer **ORG** for the source recording.

Use [Mixer and effects](/app/mixer-effects) for level and tone.
