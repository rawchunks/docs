Rawchunks saves unfinished editing work as you go. **Undo** and **Redo** on the waveform control bar handle recent arrangement changes.

## Save a milestone

1. Select **COMMIT** on the waveform bar or in **HISTORY**.
2. Enter a short description, such as “Before the chorus rework.”
3. Select **SAVE SNAPSHOT** with your account connected.
4. Open **HISTORY → COMMITS** to see the saved milestone.

## Restore a snapshot

Choose a commit under **COMMITS**, then **RESTORE**. The editor applies saved track settings and tempo. Existing clips and additional lanes can remain, so this is not a complete rollback. Commit your current work first if you want a milestone to return to.

## Inspect takes

**TAKES** lists the available candidates. Select one to reveal **PLAY**, **MOUNT**, and its delete control. **MOUNT** becomes **BASE** when active. Rework and extracted takes mount for comparison; a Layer switches the active version of its instrument lane. Neither action merges audio into Master.

A snapshot saves an arrangement state. [Merging](/app/takes-merge) keeps chosen audio in Master; [downloading](/app/library#download-a-song) retrieves the song file.
