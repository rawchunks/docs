Rawchunks is a music workstation in your browser. Describe a track, add lyrics if you want vocals, then generate audio. Open the result in the editor to change a section, add an instrument, separate stems, or build parts with keys, drums, samples, and live code.

You can keep working in your library, publish a track to the Market, or buy a license to remix someone else's original.

## Start with the work you want to do

| You want to… | Start here |
| --- | --- |

| Write vocals and arrange verses or hooks | [Use the lyrics canvas](/app/lyrics) |

| Publish an original and offer remix licenses | [Publish a track](/app/publish) |

If you're a developer making music for your own product, these same tools let you create and edit the audio in the app.

## Find your way around

**STUDIO** is where you create tracks. **MY-PAGE** holds your songs, available purchased licenses, albums, and voices. **MARKET** is the public catalog. The editor opens from a song's **EDIT** action.

The bottom player lets you listen while moving between these pages. The header holds **CONNECT**, the network picker, and **SESSION** spending controls.

[Set up your account](/app/account), then [check service prices](/app/payments) before your first generation.
