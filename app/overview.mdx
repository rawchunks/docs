Rawchunks is a music workstation in your browser. Start with a sentence, an image, or a hum or spoken idea. The Songwriter Agent can prepare your song's title, musical brief, lyrics, settings, and cover art. Review the draft or fill the controls yourself, then generate audio.

Open the result in the editor to change a section, add an instrument or backing arrangement, separate stems, build parts with keys, drums, samples, and live code, or sculpt the waveform and add effects.

You can keep working in your library, publish a track to the Market, or buy a license to remix someone else's original.

## Find your way around

**STUDIO** is where you create tracks. **VIBE** prepares a song from your idea; **CONTROL** lets you edit its fields and generate audio. **MY-PAGE** holds your songs, available purchased licenses, albums, and voices. **MARKET** is the public catalog. The editor opens from **EDIT** in Studio or **AUDIO EDITOR** in a library song's menu.

The bottom player lets you listen while moving between these pages. The header holds **CONNECT**, the network picker, and **SESSION** spending controls.
