Use **Layer** when you want a new instrument alongside the source: a bass part, percussion, or a piano answer to a melody.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what should guide it">
    Master is the starting reference. Use **L** on an ordinary lane to choose it as a Layer reference. Choosing an ordinary lane removes Master from the reference set; you can choose several lanes together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the new part">
    Open **STUDIO → Layer**. Under **ADD AN INSTRUMENT**, enter **INSTRUMENT**, then **SOUND DESCRIPTION**. Both are required.

    You can also supply **Song description** and **Instrument groups (comma separated)**. Use these for context; keep the new part's request in Sound Description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the layer">
    Layer currently uses **Flexible** processing. Select **LAYER** and wait for its take.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To hear the new part alongside the song, mount it with **BASE**, keep **ORG** and **TK#** on, and adjust its placement. The [take controls](/app/takes-merge) explain how to compare and merge it.
