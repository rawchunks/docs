Use **LAYER** for one new part: a bass line, percussion, or a guitar answer between vocal lines.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the reference">
    Start with Master, or use **L** on source lanes to choose what should guide the part. Choosing an ordinary lane removes Master from the reference set; you can combine several reference lanes. Select a range if you only need part of the song.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the instrument">
    Open **LAYER → STEM**. Choose an instrument preset or enter a **CUSTOM INSTRUMENT TARGET**, such as cello or sax. Describe what it should play in the prompt.

    **Vocals** and **Backing Vocals** open the lyrics editor. Write the words for the new vocal part there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and listen">
    Select **LAYER**. It uses **Flexible** mode. The result appears in its instrument lane with a numbered take selector.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choose **ORG** to hear an existing source stem, or a numbered take to hear the generated part. The lane's **Merge Layer** checkmark keeps that take in the lane. To combine a layer with Master, use the [take controls](/app/takes-merge#layer-on-master).
