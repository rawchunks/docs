**EXTRACT** appears in two places. Beside a selected waveform range, it cuts an audio slice. In AI Studio, it isolates instruments.

## Extract a section

1. Focus Master or a source stem and select the range.
2. Select **EXTRACT** beside **AUDITION** and **REWORK**.
3. Wait for the take and WAV download. Find the take in the matrix or **+N** vault.

  <img src="/images/app/editor-timeline.jpg" alt="Selected range with the Extract action beside Audition and Rework." />
</Frame>

The slice uses the source audio. It does not export the complete arrangement with every added lane and effect.

## Isolate instruments

1. Choose **EXTRACT** in AI Studio.
2. Open **TARGET**, or select a visible quick target.
3. Choose **All**, **Vocals**, **Drums**, **Bass**, **Other**, or **Instrumental**. Additional targets include backing vocals, guitar, keyboard, percussion, strings, synth, FX, brass, and woodwinds.
4. Select **EXTRACT** and wait for the result.
5. Choose a resulting lane in the matrix or vault. Use its play icon to check it, then **M**, **S**, and [Mixer](/app/mixer-effects) to balance it.

<Frame caption="Example project in the current Rawchunks app.">
  <img src="/images/app/editor-separate.jpg" alt="Extract settings dialog with standard stem targets and specific instrument targets." />
</Frame>

New stems can start muted. Check **M** before arrangement playback. The matrix's **SPLIT STEMS** shortcut requests all stems when no extracted stems exist.

Both actions use your [session budget](/app/payments#service-prices).
