Select the part you want to change on the waveform, then **REWORK**. You can also open **STUDIO** in the toolbox and choose **Rework**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the change">
    Enter **SOUND DESCRIPTION** or use the toolbox's **Describe the music…** field. Describe the new part: “A dry snare and sparse bass under the vocal,” for example. Add optional **LYRICS** for the section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how much to change">
    Under **REWORK INTENSITY & MODE**, choose **Conservative**, **Balanced**, or **Aggressive**. **STRENGTH** is adjustable only in Balanced, from **0–100%**.

    **Transition length (seconds)** offers **0–10 seconds** to guide the join around the changed section. Listen to the join after processing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the take">
    Choose an available **Flexible**, **Detailed**, or **Fast** processing mode in the toolbox header. Select **REWORK** and wait for the operation to finish.
  </Step>
</Steps>
