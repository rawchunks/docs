Select a range, then **REWORK** beside it. You can also choose **REWORK** in AI Studio.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the change">
    Enter the new part in the prompt: “A dry snare and sparse bass under the vocal,” for example. The prompt's expand icon gives you more writing space. Open **LYRICS** if the section needs different words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the intensity">
    Open **INTENSITY**. Choose **Subtle (0%)**, **Light (25%)**, **Balanced (50%)**, **Strong (75%)**, or **Bold (100%)**, or adjust the slider between them. Lower values keep more of the source; higher values request a larger change.

    **BOUNDARY SEAM CROSSFADE** covers **0–2 seconds**. Use it to adjust the join around the section, then listen to that boundary in the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the take">
    Choose an available **Flexible**, **Detailed**, or **Fast** mode. Select **REWORK** and wait for the take.
  </Step>
</Steps>
