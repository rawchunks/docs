---
title: "Import or record a sample"
sidebarTitle: "Sampler"
description: "Preview audio, choose a slice, and add it as a lane."
---

Open **SAMPLE** in the manual dock or **+N** menu.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose audio">
    Drop one file into the importer or select its browse area. It accepts **WAV**, **MP3**, **FLAC**, and **OGG**; playback depends on your browser's audio support.

    To record a microphone, select **RECORD**, allow microphone access, then stop when finished.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a slice">
    **PREVIEW** plays the full recording. Choose **2**, **4**, **8**, or **16 SLICES**, then select a slice to hear and choose it. Full-file Preview clears the slice selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import the part">
    Select **IMPORT**. The app saves the audio and adds the chosen slice, or the whole recording, as a sample lane at the start of the timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**REPLACE** chooses another file. Slices divide the recording equally; they do not detect drum hits automatically.

For a vocal reference used in generation, use [Voice](/app/voices). The waveform add menu's **AUDIO** choice adds a source-song clip; **SAMPLER** opens this importer for your own audio.
