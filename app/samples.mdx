---
title: "Import or record a sample"
sidebarTitle: "Sampler"
description: "Preview audio, divide it into equal slices, and add a file or slice as a lane."
---

Open **SAMPLER** in the toolbox.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose audio">
    Drop one file into the importer or select the browse area. The panel lists **WAV**, **MP3**, **FLAC**, and **OGG**; playback depends on your browser's audio support.

    To record a microphone, select **RECORD VOICE**, allow microphone access, then **STOP** when finished.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview or choose a slice">
    Use **PREVIEW** for the full recording. Choose **SLICES: 2, 4, 8, or 16** to divide it equally, then select a slice to hear and choose it. Full-file Preview clears the slice selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the part">
    Select **ADD TO WORKSTATION**. This uploads the audio and adds the chosen slice, or the whole recording, as a new sample lane at the start of the timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="A local example file loaded into the Rawchunks Sampler.">
  <img src="/images/app/editor-sampler.jpg" alt="Sampler showing file duration, slice count buttons, preview, change file and Add to Workstation." />

**CHANGE FILE** chooses another source. The slices are equal divisions, not automatically detected drum hits.

Microphone recording here is ordinary audio recording. For a vocal reference or trained voice used in generation, use [Custom Vocal](/app/voices).

The board's **AUDIO** button adds another source-song clip. Use **SAMPLER** when you want to import your own file.
