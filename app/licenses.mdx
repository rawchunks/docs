A Rawchunks commercial license gives your account stem access and the right to create minted derivatives and remixes through the app. It isn't used up when you make a remix: you can make multiple remixes from the same licensed original.

## Buy a license

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an original">
    Open **MARKET** and find a song with **BUY LICENSE** enabled. Connect your account and select the song's network.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the price">
    Select **BUY LICENSE**. Check the song and price in the dialog. The confirmed onchain license price is used for the purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the purchase">
    Select **CONFIRM PURCHASE** or **PAY WITH CARD** and review any token approval, account activation, or purchase prompts. Wait for ownership confirmation before continuing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The button is unavailable when no licenses remain or the song's license can't be verified. If you already own the license, Market hides **BUY LICENSE**; use My Page to remix it.

## Open a remix

1. Open **MY-PAGE → MY LICENSES**.
2. Select the original's network and select the remix icon.
3. The app opens a new remix in the editor. Use the [editing tools](/app/editor) to change it.
4. [Publish](/app/publish) or [download](/app/library#download-a-song) it when ready. Either can mint the remix and link it to the original.

<Frame caption="The remix entry for an example purchased original in My Page.">
  <img src="/images/app/licensed-remix.jpg" alt="My Licenses section with the original song and its remix action." />
</Frame>

**MY LICENSES** checks the current public originals. A song can disappear from that list if its creator removes the public listing, even though your license remains owned.

## What is sold

Licenses are offered from the original's fixed allocation. Each purchase leaves fewer available licenses. For license payments, **80% goes to the original creator and 20% to the Rawchunks treasury**. On a $5 license, that is $4 and $1.

Remixes show **REMIX** and cannot be licensed or remixed again in the app.

The purchase dialog describes stem access and minted derivatives or remixes. It does not state an exclusive license or transfer of copyright ownership.
