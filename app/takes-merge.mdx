A **take** is a candidate from an edit or extraction. Mounting puts it in the arrangement for comparison. Merging keeps the chosen audio.

Full-song Restyle results use **#TK1**, **#TK2**, and the other numbered buttons beside **MASTER**. Choose **MASTER** to return to the base mix.

## Mount a section on Master

For a Rework or extracted section:

1. Select a take in the matrix, **+N** vault, or **HISTORY → TAKES**.
2. Use its play icon to hear it alone. Numbered pills choose between candidates.
3. Select **BASE** on the focused take. It becomes a checkmark when mounted. In History, the same action is **MOUNT**, then **BASE**.
4. Select the small **MST** horizon to return to Master. The mounted section appears on its waveform; the **TK#** button enables or silences that take.

## Layer on Master

A generated Layer plays in its own instrument lane alongside Master. Select its numbered take, keep Master unmuted, and use the top transport to hear them together. Use **M** to compare the mix with and without the layer.

The lane's **Merge Layer** checkmark keeps that take in the instrument lane. It does not fold the layer into Master; **Merge all** does not include these lanes.

## Merge all

On Master's control bar, select the stacked-layer icon, **Merge all mounted sections**. Enable the takes you want to include and listen before merging.

</Frame>

This keeps Master and the enabled mounted takes. Ordinary Keys, Drums, Code, and sample lanes are not included automatically.

## Merge a section

The current layout does not expose the individual **Merge**, **ORG**, position reset, and discard controls after you select a mounted section. Use **Merge all** for the enabled mounted takes. A smaller waveform selection alone does not make **Merge all** merge only that selection.

## Unmount or remove a take

Select the take's mounted checkmark again to unmount it and keep the candidate. **HISTORY → TAKES** offers a delete control for candidates you no longer want.

**Reset Master** returns to the available original mix and resets its wave sculpting. Use [History](/app/history) for saved arrangement milestones.
