Open **STUDIO**. It starts in **VIBE**, where the Songwriter Agent prepares a song from text, an image, or a hum or spoken brief. Choose **CONTROL** to fill the form yourself.

You need a [connected account](/app/account), the signed disclaimer, and an [active spending session](/app/payments) to run Songwriter or generate audio.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the song">
    Follow [Start from a vibe](/app/vibe) to have Songwriter fill the form. It opens **CONTROL** as the draft arrives.

    To write your own brief, open **CONTROL → DETAILS**. Enter a music **PROMPT** or paste **LYRICS**. At least one needs three characters. For an instrumental, enable **INSTRUMENTAL** and enter a prompt; the lyric and language controls are disabled.

    With lyrics empty, Rawchunks can write them from your prompt. Review the words before using the finished track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    In **DETAILS**, check the title, prompt, [lyrics and language](/app/lyrics), and [cover or album](/app/covers-albums).

    Open **MORE OPTIONS** for [style tags, duration, tempo, key and scale](/app/music-settings). Add a [custom voice](/app/voices) if needed. **FINANCIALS** holds the [song's selling terms](/app/publish#choose-selling-terms).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a mode and generate">
    Use the mode selector at the top of Studio to choose **FAST** or **MASTER**, then select **GENERATE AUDIO** below the form. This submits the job and uses your session budget. [Service prices](/app/payments#service-prices) differ between modes; Songwriter is a separate charge.

    Wait for the musical draft to finish. You can generate while cover artwork is still preparing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Listen and keep working

Follow the item in **VARIATIONS_FEED** until it is **READY**. Select **PLAY** to listen, **EDIT** to open the workstation, **SAVE VOICE** to keep its vocals as a [custom voice](/app/voices), or **MAKE PUBLIC** to [publish it](/app/publish).

New tracks begin with a private app listing. Leaving Studio does not cancel submitted processing.

On a phone, **CREATE** and **FEED** switch between the form and the generation feed. A submitted generation switches to **FEED**.
