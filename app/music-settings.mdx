Open **STUDIO → CONTROL → GLOBAL PARAMETERS**. Each parameter button opens its own controls; **DONE** closes them after your selection. If you used [Vibe](/app/vibe), review the choices Songwriter filled here.

## Style tags

Choose up to five: **PUNCHY 808 BASS**, **WARM ANALOG SYNTH**, **CRISP ACOUSTIC DRUMS**, **AIRY LEAD VOCALS**, **LUSH BACKING HARMONIES**, **FUNKY ELECTRIC GUITAR**, **CINEMATIC STRINGS**, or **LO-FI TAPE SATURATION**. Select a tag again to remove it.

## Duration

**DURATION** offers **30, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240, or 300 seconds**. The custom slider covers **15–300 seconds** in five-second steps.

The button shows **120s (DEFAULT)** when unset. **RESET** clears your explicit target. Listen and check the completed track's length; the label is not a promise of an exact output duration.

## Tempo

**TEMPO (BPM)** offers **80**, **110**, **128**, **140**, **160**, and **174 BPM**, plus a custom slider from **40–220 BPM**. **AUTO** clears the target.

## Key and scale

In **KEY & SCALE**, choose a root from **C** through **B**, including sharps, then **major**, **minor**, **dorian**, **phrygian**, or **mixolydian**. **AUTO** removes the target.

Tempo, key, and scale guide generation. Check the audio before using it in a project that needs a precise match.

## Vocal language and voice

**LANGUAGE** is in **CONTROL → DETAILS**, beside **LYRICS**. Search by language name or code, select a result, then **DONE**. **AUTO** clears the language choice. The service supplies the available language list.

**VOICE** is in Global Parameters. See [custom voices](/app/voices) for reference uploads, saved voices, and the resemblance control.
