Open **SESSION** to fund generation, online edits, voice preparation, and listening. Choose **USDC**, **CREDIT CARD**, or an available **CAMPAIGN** offer. **1 Credit = $0.01**.

## Service prices

| Action | USD | Credits |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Generate with **FAST** | $0.05 | 5 |
| Generate with **MASTER** (Studio Master) | $0.10 | 10 |
| **Rework**, **Layer**, **Accompany**, **Extend**, or **Restyle** | $0.02 | 2 |
| Isolate stems or instruments with **EXTRACT** | $0.02 | 2 |
| Extract a selected source section | $0.02 | 2 |
| **SAVE VOICE** from a song | $0.05 | 5 |
| Upload a vocal reference | $0.05 | 5 |

A new vocal upload is charged separately from generation. Voice preparation is included in saving the sample; there is no separate training purchase.

### USDC

Connect your account and choose a payment network. Select **USDC** in Session, then set the cap: **100 ($1)**, **500 ($5)**, **1K ($10)**, **2.5K ($25)**, or **5K ($50)**. The custom field takes **Credits**, so enter **1000** for a $10 cap.

Choose **1 HOUR**, **6 HOURS**, **24 HOURS**, or **7 DAYS**. The default is **1K ($10) for 24 hours**. Select **START SESSION** and review the approval or account-activation prompts.

The cap authorizes spending; your funds stay in your wallet until used. Service payments use x402. A session belongs to one account and network.

### Credit card and prepaid Credits

Select **CREDIT CARD**, choose **1K ($10)**, **2.5K ($25)**, or **10K ($100)**, then complete the offered checkout. If you already have an available balance, **USE CREDITS** starts a session from it.

Unspent prepaid Credits return to your available balance when that session ends. Card checkout availability depends on the options offered in your account.

### Campaign

The welcome campaign offers **100 free Credits ($1)** to the first **1,000 eligible users**, once per account. It requires a verified social, email, or passkey login. Select **CAMPAIGN** and claim the offer when available.

Campaign Credits cover AI song generation and stem editing. They cannot pay another creator for streaming. The campaign starts a six-hour session.

## Listening and selling

Listening to another creator's track uses its listed **$/MIN** rate and your active session. Your own tracks are exempt. Check the rate before playing; pausing stops listening time from increasing.

Song selling terms and commercial license purchases are separate from these service fees. See [publishing](/app/publish) and [licenses](/app/licenses).

## Check or end a session

Reopen **SESSION** to check remaining, used, reserved, and total budget plus expiration. Refresh for current status. **END SESSION** closes the session. It does not remove a token approval from your wallet.
