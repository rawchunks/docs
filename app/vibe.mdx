Open **STUDIO → VIBE**. Give the Songwriter Agent an idea and it fills the musical form for you. You can use text, an image, a recording, or combine them.

## Describe an idea

Write in **PROMPT** as you would describe the song to someone. Include a story or mood, and any musical choices you already know. For example:

“A late-night synth pop song about missing the last train home. Airy vocals in English, 90 seconds, around 110 BPM.”

Ask for an instrumental if you want music without vocals. Name the vocal language if it matters to you. You can leave Prompt empty when you attach an image or record a clip.

## Use an image

Select **UPLOAD IMAGE** under **IMAGE PROMPT**, or drop an image into that area. Add text to explain the story or sound you want from it.

Select the image preview to replace it. Its **×** removes it. Songwriter uses the image's mood and theme to shape the song and prepares separate cover artwork.

## Hum or speak

1. Select **HUM / SPEAK** and allow microphone access.
2. Hum a musical idea, sing a phrase, or say what you want the song to sound like.
3. Select **STOP** when finished. Recording stops automatically at **30 seconds**.
4. Select the clip's play icon to preview it. **MIC** records again; **×** clears it.

The recording guides the song's rhythm, mood, and musical shape. Spoken or sung words can also inform the brief. For a particular vocal sound in the generated track, choose a [custom voice](/app/voices).

## Run Songwriter and review

Select the lower **VIBE** button. You need a connected account, the signed disclaimer, and an active session. Check the [Songwriter price](/app/payments#service-prices) before running it.

Studio opens **CONTROL** and shows progress while fields arrive. Songwriter can write the title, production prompt, and tagged lyrics, choose vocals or instrumental, and set language, style tags, tempo, key, scale, and duration. It also prepares cover art.

<Frame caption="Example Songwriter progress in Control. Audio generation becomes available when the musical draft is ready.">

When the musical draft is ready, review **DETAILS** and **MORE OPTIONS**. Cover artwork can finish while you review or generate the audio. Open the [lyrics canvas](/app/lyrics) to change words or sections. Choose your voice, album, and selling terms where needed.

When you're ready, [generate the audio](/app/generate). To try another idea, return to **VIBE** and change your inputs. A new run can replace the draft fields, lyrics, and cover.
