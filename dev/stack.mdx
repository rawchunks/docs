Use HTTPS REST for music and project data, multipart for uploads, and NDJSON for job progress and agent output. Your product can use these interfaces with its own UI.

| Work | Interface |
| --- | --- |
| Generate, edit and prepare voices | Fastify API, asynchronous job IDs and status or NDJSON. |
| Prepare a song or Code pattern | Songwriter and Composer requests with streamed output. |
| Play, sequence and process effects | Client audio engine. Rawchunks uses Zustand, Tone.js, Web Audio and supported Strudel notation. |
| Save projects and media | Songs, takes, stems, albums, history, WIP and returned audio URLs. |
| Pay for services | USDC sessions or purchased/promotional Credits. |
| Mint or purchase onchain | Wallet calls to Rawchunks contracts and programs. |

Read [AI/ML stack](/ai-ml/stack) for the model workers, L4s, compiled inference, databases and storage. [Concurrency](/ai-ml/concurrency) explains queueing and replica scaling.

## IDs and units

Store song, stem, take, task and voice IDs as returned. Chain song IDs are decimal strings; convert them to `bigint` for contract calls. Durations and timeline bounds use seconds. Payment amounts use six-decimal atomic units: `1000000` equals one USD or USDC.

## Integration sequence

[Set up requests](/dev/guides/connect), choose [funding](/dev/guides/payments), then submit a music or agent operation. Keep the returned IDs and follow [job progress](/dev/guides/jobs). Your editor previews candidates and persists the chosen take or rendered mix.

Publishing continues through the [minting](/dev/guides/publish/mint) and [license](/dev/guides/publish/licenses) interfaces.
