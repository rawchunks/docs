Use `primaryUrl` only when the body says `status: "ready"`. Keep the original task/song pair. A mismatched pair returns 409.

[Polling and streams](/dev/guides/jobs).

<ParamField path="taskId" type="string" required>

</ParamField>

<ParamField query="songId" type="string" required>

</ParamField>
