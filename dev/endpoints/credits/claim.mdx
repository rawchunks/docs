Claim the campaign allocation for an eligible account.

Use [request setup](/dev/guides/connect) for account headers.

## Headers

Send `x-wallet-auth-token`, `x-session-user-address` and `x-idempotency-key` (8 to 128 characters).

## Response

Returns the balance response. The campaign offers 100 Credits to the first 1,000 eligible accounts. Reuse the same key for the same claim; eligibility and rate limits can reject a request.
