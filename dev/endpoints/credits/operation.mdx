api: "GET https://api.rawchunks.space/api/credits/operations/{id}"
playground: "simple"
---

Recover the status of a Credits or license operation.

Use [request setup](/dev/guides/connect) for account headers.

## Authentication

Use [Credits authentication](/dev/guides/payments#pay-with-credits) for owner-scoped calls.

## Path

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
Operation ID, 1 to 128 characters.
</ParamField>


## Response

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">
Operation ID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
Current payment state.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="amountAtomics" type="string">
Six-decimal amount.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="orderId" type="string or null">
Provider order ID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="kind" type="string">
Purchase or license operation.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="coinflowPaymentId" type="string or null">
License checkout payment ID.
</ResponseField>
