Read bipolar waveform peaks without decoding the whole track in your client.

Use [request setup](/dev/guides/connect) for account headers.

## Query

<ParamField query="url" type="string" required>
Audio URL.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="bins" type="integer">
16 to 2048; defaults to 512.
</ParamField>


## Response

<ResponseField name="mins" type="number[]">
Minimum amplitudes per bin.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="maxs" type="number[]">
Maximum amplitudes per bin.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="duration" type="number">
Audio duration in seconds.
</ResponseField>
