Recover words from audio or align supplied lyrics to it.

Use [request setup](/dev/guides/connect) for account headers.

## Path

<ParamField path="songId" type="string" required>
Owned, editable song with audio.
</ParamField>


## JSON body

<ParamField body="vocalLanguage" type="string">
Vocal language code.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="lyrics" type="string">
Words to align.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="mode" type="string">
`extract` or `align`. Defaults to align when lyrics are supplied, otherwise extract.
</ParamField>


## Response

<ResponseField name="lyrics" type="string">
Recovered or supplied words.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="vocalLanguage" type="string">
Resolved language.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="alignment" type="object or null">
Timed lyric alignment.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
`cached` or `none`.
</ResponseField>


## Source

Uses the vocal stem when available, then the master or song audio.
