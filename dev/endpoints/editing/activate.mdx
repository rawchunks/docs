`base` selects the take. A Layer candidate becomes a new unmuted stem. `merge` without `composition` merges the take’s recorded region. With `composition`, upload your rendered WAV and supply the latest history `commitId`.

[Takes](/dev/guides/edit/takes) · [Client-rendered merge](/dev/guides/edit/merge).

<RequestExample>

```bash cURL
curl --fail-with-body -X POST "$RAWCHUNKS_API/api/editing/activate" \
  -H "x-session-user-address: $USER_ADDRESS" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data "$(jq -nc --arg user "$USER_ADDRESS" --arg song "$SONG_ID" --arg take "$TAKE_ID" '{
  "songId": $song,
  "userAddress": $user,
  "takeId": $take,
  "action": "base"
}')"
```
