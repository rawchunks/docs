Normalize tempo, key, duration and vocal language.

Use [request setup](/dev/guides/connect) for account headers.

## JSON body

<ParamField body="bpm" type="number">
Round and clamp to 40 to 220.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="keyScale" type="string">
Root and scale; flat roots normalize to sharp equivalents.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="audioDuration" type="number">
Round to five-second steps and clamp to 15 to 300.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="vocalLanguage" type="string">
Supported two-letter language code; defaults to en.
</ParamField>


## Response

Returns normalized `bpm`, `keyScale`, `audioDuration` and `vocalLanguage`. Unusable tempo, key or duration values can be omitted. An unsupported scale becomes minor; an unsupported language becomes en.
