Turn a hum, sung phrase or spoken recording into a musical brief.

Use [request setup](/dev/guides/connect) for account headers.

## JSON body

<ParamField body="audio" type="string" required>
Audio data URL or base64 recording.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="prompt" type="string">
Additional written brief.
</ParamField>


## Response

<ResponseField name="prompt" type="string">
Combined musical brief.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="features" type="object">
`durationSec`, `bpm`, `keyScale`, `scaleMode`, `meanPitchHz`, `pitchRangeSemitones`, `contour`, `groove`, `onsetRatePerSec`, `energyLevel`, `energyTrend`, `vocalTone`, `mood`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="stt" type="object">
`text`, `language`, `isLexical`, optional `flaggedAs`, and `provider` (`cartesia`, `elevenlabs`, `none`).
</ResponseField>
