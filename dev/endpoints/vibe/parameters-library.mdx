Read the musical controls used by Songwriter.

Use [request setup](/dev/guides/connect) for account headers.

## Response

<ResponseField name="parameters" type="object">
BPM, key and scale, duration presets, vocal languages and instrumental choice.
</ResponseField>


## Ranges

The library describes BPM 40 to 220 and duration presets from 30 to 300 seconds. [Normalization](/dev/endpoints/vibe/parameters) also accepts intermediate durations down to 15 seconds.
