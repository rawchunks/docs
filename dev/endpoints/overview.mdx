## Route aliases

`/vibe/*`, `/strudel/*` and `/voices/*` expose the same service contracts as their `/api` forms. Profile updates also accept `PUT` and `POST`; consent and guide writes also accept `PUT`.

Editing supports `/api/editing/commit` for commits, `/api/editing/takes/activate` for activation, and `/api/editing/reset` with a body `songId`. Listening supports `/api/stream/{id}` and `/api/stream/{id}/checkpoint`. PayPal notifications also use `/api/payments/paypal/webhook`.

The top-level `/generate`, `/status/{taskId}`, `/songs`, `/songs/{id}`, `/songs/{id}/stream`, `/stream/{id}`, `/sample`, `/format`, `/repaint`, `/layer`, `/extend`, `/remix`, `/isolate`, `/reset`, `/voice` and account paths forward to the corresponding service contracts.

[Onchain calls](/dev/guides/publish/contract-reference) use wallet contracts and programs separately from REST.
