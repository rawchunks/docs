License payments send 80% to the recorded creator and 20% to the configured treasury. In atomic units:

```javascript
const treasuryFee = price * 2_000n / 10_000n;
const creatorShare = price - treasuryFee;
```

Integer division rounds the treasury fee down; the creator receives the remainder. A price of `1_000_001` splits into `800_001` for the creator and `200_000` for the treasury.
