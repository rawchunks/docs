Paid requests accept [USDC session or Credits headers](/dev/guides/payments). Use the selected account consistently in headers and creator fields.

<ParamField header="Authorization" type="string">
For USDC: `Bearer` followed by the complete issued session token.
</ParamField>

For Credits, send `x-wallet-auth-token` and `x-credit-source` (`card` or `promo`). A stable `x-idempotency-key` of at most 80 characters identifies a paid service operation.
